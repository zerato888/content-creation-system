# Which content format drove the most newsletter signups in Q1?

## Question

Of the three content formats produced in Q1 (carousels, short-form
video, long-form articles), which drove the most newsletter signups?

## Answer

Short-form video drove roughly 2x the signups of carousels and articles
combined, despite being the smallest share of total output by volume.
[LIKELY — based on link-in-bio click tracking, not a controlled test]

## Method / sources

Compared link-in-bio click-through data across the three formats over a
90-day window. No A/B test was run — this is observational, not causal.

## Confidence

LIKELY. Confounded by short-form video also having the highest total
reach in the same period — reach and format are not cleanly separated in
this data.
